BitCadence Platform v2: low-fidelity screens

Coding, Media and AI Ops in one product. A proposal for owner review; design doc: docs/PLATFORM-V2-DESIGN.md.

00 · Home

Needs you first, then what's running. Status ladder on every card.

 01 · Projects & Create

One project with a coding, a media and an ops flow. Start from an outcome or a tested template.

 02 · Flow

An editable step list, not a node canvas. Change it in words, see every change. Checks before Start.

 03 · Run & recovery

Previews, why it's waiting, and one recovery action: what happened, what's saved, what you can do.

 04 · Review & approve

Technical checks apart from taste. Compare takes, comment at a timecode, fix just one part, approve one exact version.

 05 · Library & connections

Characters, voices, brand kits, repositories, and connections that show what each one may do.

 06 · Operations

Computers (model, role and computer kept apart), helpers, queue reasons, GPU owner, spend with both caps, repairs, takeovers.

 07 · Settings

Turn off asking before publishing (per flow and place), the overall spend cap, budgets for new flows, who can grant takeovers.

Rules these screens follow

  • Five places: Home, Projects, Library, Operations, Settings. These replace today's 12-item sidebar.
  • No ids, tokens, YAML, leases or node ids in normal use. "Details" shows them read-only.
  • Making, releasing and publishing each need their own approval.
  • Every state has a word, not just a colour. Tap targets are 48 px or more. Reduced motion is respected. Every screen works on a phone.
  • The redesign v1 screens still apply where they are not replaced: redesign v1.

The sample values (names, times, costs, model names) are made up for illustration and are not fleet data.
