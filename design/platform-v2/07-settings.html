Settings

The few things only you decide. Everything else has a safe default.

Publishing

Publishing always waits for your OK, unless you turn that off below for one flow and one destination.

FlowWherePublish without asking me
Publish an episodeYouTube
Release MyMealsProduction

Only you can turn this on. Helpers and the Chief can't. When it's on, those runs show "Publishes automatically" before they start. Creative review, prayer sign-off and checks still happen.

Spend

Each project also has its own cap in Operations › Spend. A paid step has to fit both.

Trying new flows

A new flow that uses paid tools asks you the first time. When you OK it, you also set a budget for running it again.

FlowBudget for re-runsLeft
Thumbnail variations (experimental, v1)$5.00$3.20

If the flow's steps change, it asks you again. A budget never carries over to a changed flow.

Who can let a helper take over

  1. The Chief (Claude desktop, or its sidecar when the desktop app is closed)
  2. The Deputy Chief: Grokbot, if the Chief isn't available
  3. You: you can always grant, revoke or override

A takeover covers stopping and moving jobs in one run, for up to 4 hours. It can't approve, publish, spend over a cap, or hand out more takeovers. A seat can't grant a takeover to itself. Grokbot also reviews work, so it never reviews work touched by a takeover it granted.

Everything else

Pause everything, start with my computer, lock this computer down: unchanged from redesign v1.
