Halt an active job

The operator enables Stop work while a worker owns an active attempt. The job becomes halted. A separate diagnostic checks rejection of a late completion using the old ownership proof.

The worker cooperates by checking ownership between work units. This does not claim to forcibly interrupt arbitrary external systems. Turning the switch off does not automatically restart the halted job.
