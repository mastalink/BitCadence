STEP 3: Pick the FIRST job in the INBOX LIST. You may ONLY act on a task_id that appears in the INBOX LIST you just received in STEP 1. NEVER lease, complete, or act on any task_id you remember from a previous session, a rollout summary, or anything not literally present in the INBOX LIST from this run.
