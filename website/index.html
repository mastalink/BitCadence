Drumline · shared memory

What one agent learns,
every agent knows.

Drumline is the memory every player reads and writes, across vendors and over time. A Claude session learns something on Monday; a Codex or Gemini worker opens its prompt on Tuesday with that fact already in it. Finished jobs are distilled into handoffs automatically, so the band never starts from silence.

Auto-distilled handoffs

When a job completes, what was asked and what came back becomes a memory entry, then rides into the next agent's prompt.

Deterministic and auditable

Recall is a plain score: term overlap, entry weight, recency, role. No embeddings, no vector-database bill, and you can see why an entry was chosen.

Local, in the free edition

An embedded SQLite store on your machine. Recalled entries are framed as reference data, never as instructions. Sharing across machines is part of the Team edition.

Decision models

A quick judgment, never a blank check.

Plug in Jev from TypeSafe, or any typed decision model with an API. It answers small questions fast: who should take this job, is this alert noise, which evidence matters. Each answer is typed and carries a confidence. It starts in shadow mode, where it only annotates, and deterministic policy and human approvals stay in charge.

Every judgment leaves a receipt in the audit trail. If the model is down or abstains, a deterministic fallback runs; it is never permission to skip a gate. The values above are illustrative, showing the shape of a judgment rather than a measured result.

Questions

Plain answers.

Is it free?

For personal and noncommercial use, yes. The source is available under the Prosperity Public License 3.0.0. Businesses should read the commercial license.

Which models does it work with?

Any. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok are the first chairs, Qwen, GLM, DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral and Kimi fill the ranks, and any hosted or local model from any vendor can join the band.

Does my data leave my machine?

BitCadence runs locally and keeps its shared memory in an embedded store on your machine. What your agents send to their model vendors depends on the models you choose.

Who approves what?

You do. Risky actions wait in an approval queue, and every approval is written to the audit trail.
